# Home 3D Card

A 3D dollhouse view of your home with tappable, glowing light entities.

- Lights glow when on (intensity follows brightness) and dim when off.
- Tap a light in the 3D view to toggle it.
- Visual editor: click on the model to place light markers, assign entities,
  set colour/size, and drag to reposition.

Export your home from Sweet Home 3D to GLB (see README), drop it in
`config/www/`, and point the card at `/local/your-home.glb`.
